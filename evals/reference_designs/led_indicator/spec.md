# Power indicator LED on a 5 V supply

5 V comes in on a two-pin header. Light an LED (forward voltage 2.0 V,
give or take 10%) through a 330 ohm 1% resistor rated 0.1 W. The resistor
must run at no more than half its rating.

Nothing in this design sets the 5 V rail's tolerance, so the resistor's
worst-case power cannot be established and Kairo must say so.
