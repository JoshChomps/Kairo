# Non-inverting amplifier with a gain of two

An MCP6001 op-amp on a single 3.3 V supply amplifies a signal by two:
the signal goes to the non-inverting input, a 10k 1% resistor joins the
output to the inverting input and another the inverting input to ground.
100 nF sits across the supply. The signal comes in on one two-pin header
and goes out on another; 3.3 V comes in on a third.
